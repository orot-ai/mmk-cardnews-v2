# 대한민국 벚꽃명소 — Research

## Topic Overview

South Korea's cherry blossom season runs from late March to mid-April each year, drawing millions of domestic and international visitors. The season typically begins in the south (Jeju, Busan, Jinhae) and moves northward to Seoul. Each region offers a distinct experience — from centuries-old historic settings to modern urban parks and coastal landscapes.

## Key Spots (Top 7)

### 1. 진해 군항제 (Jinhae Gunhangje) — Changwon
- Korea's largest and most famous cherry blossom festival
- Approximately 360,000 cherry trees across the city
- Iconic spots: 여좌천 벚꽃로 (Yeojwa Stream), 경화역 (Gyeonghwa Station railway with trees)
- 2026 first bloom: March 25; millions of visitors annually
- One of the world's largest cherry blossom festivals

### 2. 경주 (Gyeongju) — North Gyeongsang
- Ancient capital known as "museum without walls"
- ~9,000 cherry trees along an 8km trail at Bomun Lake
- Blossoms frame historic sites: Bulguksa Temple, royal tombs
- 2026 first bloom: March 28
- Unique contrast between ancient architecture and delicate spring flowers

### 3. 여의도 (Yeouido) — Seoul
- Seoul's most famous cherry blossom destination
- ~1,700 cherry trees along the Han River
- Popular for hanami picnics, river cruises (₩25,000–35,000)
- 2026 first bloom: April 3; peak bloom April 13–17
- Tip: Visit at sunrise to beat crowds

### 4. 제주도 (Jeju Island)
- Southernmost region, first to bloom in Korea
- Native king cherry trees (왕벚나무) — largest blossoms in Korea
- Jeju National University is a famous spot
- 2026 first bloom: March 25
- The original habitat of the native Korean cherry tree

### 5. 하동 십리벚꽃길 (Hadong 10-ri Cherry Road) — South Gyeongsang
- Historic 1,200-tree avenue planted in 1931 by local villagers
- 4km route connecting Ssanggyesa Temple to Hwagae Market
- One of Korea's oldest and most storied cherry blossom roads
- Combines spring blossoms with tea plantation scenery

### 6. 석촌호수 (Seokchon Lake) — Seoul
- 2.5km cherry blossom path with over 1,000 royal cherry trees
- Creates a pink tunnel effect with stunning water reflections
- Located near Lotte Tower, a perfect photography backdrop

### 7. 경의선 숲길 (Gyeongui Line Forest Path) — Seoul
- Converted disused railway into a 6.3km linear park
- Continuous cherry blossom corridor in western Seoul
- A tranquil urban escape alternative to crowded Yeouido

## 2026 Bloom Timeline

| Region | First Bloom | Peak Bloom |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| Jeju / Busan / Jinhae | March 25 | Late March – Early April |
| Gyeongju | March 28 | Early April |
| Seoul | April 3 | April 10–17 |

## Key Insights & Synthesis

- Korea's cherry blossom season is remarkably short — roughly 1 week per location
- The "Golden Window" for visiting multiple regions: first week of April
- Advanced booking is essential (6 months ahead for popular festivals)
- Southern regions bloom ~1–2 weeks earlier than Seoul
- Native 왕벚나무 (king cherry) found only in Jeju has the largest blooms
- Night-lit cherry blossom events (벚꽃 야경) at Seokchon Lake and Jinhae are increasingly popular
